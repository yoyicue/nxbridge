# Input adapter provenance

NXBridge input bridge and patch application scripts use the root MIT license,
except for upstream-derived material listed below. Patch context retains its
upstream copyright and license; the root MIT license does not replace it.

- Moonlight Android: [revision b48494c](https://github.com/moonlight-stream/moonlight-android/tree/b48494cb96bff23d8886c4775cc4f39a1075495d), GPL version 3. The `moonlight-pad-layout*.patch` files modify its virtual controller, `moonlight-pad-cover-325.patch` modifies game artwork sizing, `moonlight-library-cover-327.patch` adds version-aware cover refresh, and `moonlight-startup-loading-331.patch` adds the first-frame loading screen. The experimental `moonlight-layout-editor-350.patch` adds per-game layout editing, stick response and input safety work; apply it after the earlier client patches ending in 331. `moonlight-layout-validation-351.patch` follows 350 and validates projected control overlap on all layout-loading and saving paths. `moonlight-safe-area-353.patch` follows 351 and accounts for window insets and parent clipping; `moonlight-layout-commit-354.patch` follows 353 and guards final persistence against display changes. `moonlight-input-lab-356.patch` follows 354 and adds a disconnected diagnostic using the production controller parser and touch controls; `moonlight-layout-recovery-357.patch` follows 356 and preserves invalid layouts during explicit recovery; `moonlight-layout-history-359.patch` follows 357 and offers valid historical revisions for preview and recovery; `moonlight-layout-transfer-363.patch` follows 359 and adds offline portable-layout import and export; `moonlight-clean-version-365.patch` follows 363 and increments only the version after a diagnostic-only 364 experiment; `moonlight-controller-toggle-366.patch` follows 365 and restores a user-controlled display setting with a migration-safe default; `moonlight-clean-version-368.patch` follows 366 and increments only the version after a diagnostic-only 367 experiment; `moonlight-strict-layout-369.patch` follows 368 and validates local and portable layout JSON strictly; `moonlight-safe-projection-370.patch` follows 369 and adds safe-area-relative geometry with schema-1 compatibility. `moonlight-lifecycle-input-371.patch` follows 370 and adds separate lifecycle and editor input-suppression reasons; `moonlight-repeat-guard-372.patch` follows 371 but alone does not stop a held key from reappearing; `moonlight-held-guard-373.patch` follows 372 and blocks keys held during suppression until an actual release is received. `moonlight-held-help-375.patch` follows 373 and explains how to recover from a held-key block; diagnostic-only 374 is not distributed. `moonlight-compact-preset-376.patch` follows 375 and adds a validated compact candidate; `moonlight-compact-gap-377.patch` follows 376 and separates menu and shoulder hit areas. `moonlight-face-group-378.patch` follows 377 and adds safe grouped face-button movement. `moonlight-face-resize-379.patch` follows 378 and adds bounded uniform face-group scaling. `moonlight-stick-visual-380.patch` follows 379 and adds independent floating-stick visual size with schema 3; `moonlight-stick-preview-381.patch` follows 380 and adjusts the preview placement. `moonlight-stick-feedback-382.patch` follows 381 and adds optional once-per-touch edge feedback; `moonlight-haptic-guard-383.patch` follows 382 and preserves preferences when a device has no vibrator. `moonlight-startup-input-384.patch` follows 383 and suppresses controller input during loading; `moonlight-neutral-rearm-386.patch` follows 384 and rearms a physical source after neutral. Diagnostic-only 385 is not distributed. `moonlight-backup-export-387.patch` follows 386 and exports a selected damaged-layout backup through the system document picker after an explicit privacy notice. `moonlight-clean-version-389.patch` follows 387 and only increments the clean version after the diagnostic-only 388 draw experiment. `moonlight-transfer-identity-390.patch` follows 389 and binds pending layout transfers to the originating layout identity and revision. `moonlight-shoulder-group-391.patch` follows 390 and adds side-specific shoulder-pair movement in the layout editor. `moonlight-touch-owners-393.patch` follows 391 and restricts button ownership to fingers that begin inside the button, and releases the direction pad when its owning finger lifts. `moonlight-clean-version-395.patch` follows 393 and changes only the version code after the diagnostic-only 394 candidate; it does not include that candidate. `moonlight-clean-version-398.patch` follows 395 and only increments the version after diagnostic-only 396 and 397. `moonlight-button-visual-401.patch` follows 398 and adds optional smaller button graphics with unchanged hit areas and schema-4 persistence. `moonlight-cover-fit-403.patch` follows 401 and fits full artwork with a separate layout button. They remain under active validation. Changes to GPL-covered client code are distributed under GPL-3.0-only. `prepare_moonlight_390.py` replays the baseline and ordered patches into a new worktree from the pinned upstream commit; clean-machine and byte-reproducible APK builds remain unverified.
- The `tests/fixtures/client-*.patch` diagnostic patches include Moonlight Android GPL-3.0-only context and are not part of the production patch chain.
- Sunshine: [revision 63d35f7](https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine/tree/63d35f702ee9e362e43263742981836ec0710384), GPL version 3. `apply_sunshine.py` and `sunshine_bridge.hpp` provide the experimental integration; additions to the combined Sunshine program are distributed under GPL-3.0-only.
- nxnative-side C input code is NXBridge's session-scoped adapter. The separate nxnative patches retain that project's MIT notice in `../nxnative/LICENSE.upstream`.

The GPL version 3 text is included in [LICENSES/GPL-3.0-only.txt](../../LICENSES/GPL-3.0-only.txt).
